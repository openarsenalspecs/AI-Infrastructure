# ClockWork

ClockWork is an open-source, AGPL-3.0+ licensed, industry-agnostic service orchestration platform designed to replace commission-based labor markets with a transparent, time-based, ethically governed dispatch and billing system.

ClockWork enables real-time matching between clients and professionals using round-robin and intelligent routing systems, with full hourly billing, compliance enforcement, certification validation, and dispute resolution built into the core architecture.

---

## Core Concept

ClockWork operates as a **service economy operating system** where:

- Professionals sell time instead of commissions
- Clients pay transparently for hours consumed
- Work is distributed fairly through structured dispatch logic
- Every interaction is auditable, logged, and governed
- AI assists in matching, forecasting, and optimization
- Entire system is modular and industry-adaptable
