# ImpactCom

## Decentralized. Accountable. Impactful.

ImpactCom is an open-source, decentralized peer-to-peer commodity trading platform that serves as the public marketplace for [FarmLedger](https://codeberg.org/RoxanneA/FarmLedger). It enables producers, cooperatives, processors, distributors, wholesalers, manufacturers, retailers, institutions, governments, and consumers to buy and sell commodities through transparent smart contracts, secure escrow, and verified delivery workflows.

ImpactCom transforms FarmLedger from an operational farm and production management platform into a complete global commerce ecosystem by allowing every FarmLedger user to publish products for immediate sale or pre-order without maintaining duplicate inventory systems.

Designed for transparency, security, accountability, and open innovation, ImpactCom removes unnecessary intermediaries while providing a trustworthy marketplace backed by blockchain technology, AI, and decentralized infrastructure.
