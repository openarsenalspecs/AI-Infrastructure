# StreamAxis

**Built for Control at Scale.**

StreamAxis is an open-source modular platform for gyroscopic motion control, screen casting, remote interaction, display management, spatial computing, and future holographic display technologies. Designed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+), StreamAxis provides a scalable foundation for controlling, sharing, and interacting with digital content across devices, displays, and next-generation computing environments.

The platform combines motion-based input, screen streaming, remote device control, and display orchestration into a unified ecosystem that can be extended through plugins, modules, APIs, and custom hardware integrations.

## Vision

To create an open, modular platform that enables anyone to build, control, and share digital experiences across screens, projections, holograms, and future display technologies.
