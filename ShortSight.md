## Overview

**ShortSight** is an **open-source platform for financial transparency and investor accountability**.  
It allows users to submit, track, and analyze complaints related to financial advice, fund performance, and investment misconduct.  

The platform supports multiple investment sectors, including real estate, hedge funds, brokerages, and crypto.  
It aggregates complaints, tracks fund performance, monitors public statements, and flags suspicious activities to provide a **rock-solid basis for research, reporting, and legal action**.  

**ShortSight** is released under the **AGPL 3.0+ license**, ensuring that modifications and network deployments remain open and shareable.

---

## Key Features

### Complaint Management
- Submit complaints (anonymous or verified) with evidence  
- Timeline tracking and status updates  
- Link multiple complaints to the same company, advisor, or fund  
- Versioned complaint edits for transparency  
- Notes and discussion sections for researchers  

### Market & Fund Tracking
- Real-time fund performance monitoring  
- Historical trend analysis comparing advice vs. outcomes  
- Shorting activity and large capital movement tracking  
- Public statements and videos linked to complaints  

### Legal & Class Action Support
- Automatic class-action eligibility alerts  
- Pre-built legal templates for filing complaints  
- Regulatory filing notifications (SEC, FINRA, state-level)  
- Lawyer collaboration portal for verified professionals  

### Community & Collaboration
- Follow companies, advisors, and funds for alerts  
- Crowdsourced evidence verification and market analysis  
- Peer collaboration workspaces and gamified contribution rewards  
- Mentor and expert verification for credibility  

### Reporting & Analytics
- Interactive dashboards by sector, company, advisor, and investment type  
- Trend visualizations, heatmaps, and interactive timelines  
- Exportable PDF/CSV reports for legal and research purposes  
- Public reporting on industry misconduct  

### Advanced Intelligence
- AI-driven advisor reputation scoring  
- Investment risk profiling  
- Automated detection of fraud, misconduct, or misleading statements  
- Smart watchlists for advisors, companies, and sectors  

### Financial Transparency
- Advisor earnings vs. recommendation tracking  
- Conflict-of-interest alerts  
- Fund flow monitoring around investment advice events  

### Security & Data Integrity
- AES-256 encrypted evidence storage  
- Role-based access control  
- Immutable timestamps and versioned complaint/evidence storage  
- Optional blockchain-based verification

## How to Use ShortSight

1. **Submit a Complaint**  
   Use the platform interface to submit detailed complaints with supporting evidence. Tag the complaint by investment type, company, and advisor.  

2. **Track and Monitor**  
   Follow companies, advisors, or funds to receive alerts when new complaints or suspicious activity occur.  

3. **Analyze and Report**  
   Use dashboards and visualizations to track trends, fund performance, and legal eligibility for class-action complaints.  

4. **Collaborate**  
   Verified researchers and legal professionals can review evidence, link complaints, and provide expert analysis.
