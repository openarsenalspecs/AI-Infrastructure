# OpenCommonsDividend

## Returning Public Surplus to the People

OpenCommonsDividend is an open-source civic finance platform designed to identify verified financial surplus and distribute it fairly among residents, stakeholders, or organizational members.

Built as a modular system on top of Fundamenta, OpenCommonsDividend provides transparent budgeting, AI-assisted financial auditing, citizen oversight, and dividend distribution infrastructure while ensuring that all final financial decisions remain under human control.

Every automated recommendation, surplus calculation, and distribution decision must include human review and approval. The approving individual or authority must be recorded in the system to provide full traceability, accountability, and audit history.

---

# Project Vision

Financial systems often operate without sufficient transparency, making it difficult for citizens, employees, or stakeholders to understand how funds are collected, managed, and distributed.

OpenCommonsDividend creates an open infrastructure layer where surplus funds can be identified, verified, and returned to the communities or organizations that generated them.

The platform is designed around five principles:

- Transparency
- Accountability
- Fair distribution
- Human oversight
- Open infrastructure

OpenCommonsDividend can support governments, municipalities, cooperatives, nonprofits, businesses, and other organizations seeking a transparent surplus-sharing model.

---

# Modular Architecture

OpenCommonsDividend is designed as a modular platform.

The system consists of:

- Core Modules
- Optional Plug-in Modules
- Integration Modules
- Governance Modules

Each module operates independently while communicating through standardized APIs and shared data structures.

Organizations can deploy only the modules they require while expanding functionality over time.

## Surplus Engine

Calculates verified available surplus.

Features:

- Revenue analysis
- Expense analysis
- Reserve calculations
- Debt obligation calculations
- Capital project accounting
- Surplus forecasting
- Surplus verification reports

The Surplus Engine determines the available dividend pool but cannot authorize distribution without human approval.

---

## AI Surplus Auditor

Provides AI-assisted financial analysis.

Features:

- Spending anomaly detection
- Unusual transaction detection
- Vendor risk analysis
- Contract pattern analysis
- Budget variance analysis
- Cost benchmarking
- Fraud indicator detection
- Financial trend analysis

AI-generated findings are recommendations only.

All findings require human review before action.

Features:

## Development Principles

OpenCommonsDividend follows these principles:

### Human-in-the-Loop First

AI assists with analysis but does not replace human judgment.

Every final financial decision requires:

- Human approval
- Recorded identity
- Timestamp
- Decision history

---

### Modular by Design
Features should be developed as independent modules that can be enabled, disabled, or replaced.

---

### Transparency by Default
Financial calculations, approvals, and distributions should be auditable.

---

### Open Infrastructure
The platform should remain accessible, extensible, and community-driven.
