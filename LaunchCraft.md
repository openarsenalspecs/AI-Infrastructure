# LaunchCraft

LaunchCraft is an AI-powered system that helps users turn their existing skills into viable home-based business opportunities. It analyzes skill sets, scans market demand signals, identifies gaps and underserved niches, and generates actionable, step-by-step business launch plans.

The goal of LaunchCraft is not just to generate ideas, but to guide users from **skill → opportunity → execution → income** using structured intelligence rather than generic advice.

---

## 🚀 Core Concept

Most people already have monetizable skills—they just lack:
- clear market direction  
- validated opportunity identification  
- structured execution plans  

LaunchCraft solves this by mapping personal skills to real market demand and turning that mapping into practical business paths.

---

## 🧠 Key Features

### 1. Skill Intelligence Engine
- Converts user input into structured skill profiles  
- Normalizes and categorizes abilities  
- Identifies transferable and hidden skills  
- Maps skill strength and experience levels  

---

### 2. Market Opportunity Discovery
- Aggregates signals from multiple sources (forums, marketplaces, trends, job data)  
- Identifies recurring pain points and unmet needs  
- Detects underserved niches and micro-markets  
- Clusters demand signals into opportunity groups  

---

### 3. Opportunity Scoring System
- Demand strength scoring  
- Competition density evaluation  
- Entry barrier analysis  
- Differentiation potential scoring  
- Customer pain intensity indexing  
- Overall opportunity viability score  

---

### 4. Skill-to-Opportunity Matching
- Matches user capabilities with real market gaps  
- Generates personalized opportunity rankings  
- Filters based on time, budget, and risk tolerance  
- Produces ranked business pathways  

---

### 5. Business Model Generator
- Converts opportunities into structured business models  
- Supports service, product, digital, SaaS, and hybrid models  
- Defines monetization strategies  
- Suggests pricing frameworks  
- Identifies target customer segments  

---

### 6. Execution Planning System
- Generates 7-day startup plans  
- Builds 30-day launch roadmaps  
- Recommends tool stacks (free and paid)  
- Provides customer acquisition strategies  
- Outlines first revenue actions  
- Identifies common failure points  

---

### 7. Validation & Reality Filter
- Detects low-quality or unrealistic opportunities  
- Validates demand signals against market data  
- Evaluates monetization feasibility  
- Assigns confidence scores  
- Flags high-risk ideas  

---

### 8. Adaptive Learning System
- Learns from user feedback and outcomes  
- Refines scoring models over time  
- Improves recommendation accuracy  
- Tracks success/failure patterns  
- Adapts to evolving market trends  

---

### 9. Constraint-Aware Personalization
- Adjusts recommendations based on:
  - available time  
  - budget limits  
  - skill level  
  - risk tolerance  
- Filters opportunities to realistic options only  

---

### 10. Growth Path Generator
- Shows scaling trajectory from solo operation to larger business  
- Identifies automation opportunities  
- Suggests productization paths  
- Outlines long-term expansion strategies  

---

### 11. Modular AI Architecture
LaunchCraft is built as a modular system with independent components:

- Skill analysis module  
- Market ingestion module  
- Opportunity extraction module  
- Matching engine  
- Business generation layer  
- Execution planning layer  
- Validation system  

Each module can be independently improved or replaced.

---

### 12. Open-Source AGPL 3.0+ Design
- Fully transparent scoring and matching logic  
- Forkable and extensible architecture  
- Community-driven improvement model  
- Designed for network-deployed compliance under AGPL

## 📦 System Flow

User Skill Input  
→ Skill Intelligence Engine  
→ Market Opportunity Discovery  
→ Opportunity Scoring System  
→ Skill Matching Engine  
→ Business Model Generator  
→ Execution Planner  
→ Validation Layer  
→ Final Business Blueprint  

---

## 🧱 Technology Design (Conceptual)

- Backend: Python (FastAPI recommended)  
- Data Processing: Pandas / Polars  
- Vector Search: FAISS or Qdrant  
- Scraping Layer: Playwright / Scrapy  
- AI Layer: Local LLM or API-based model  
- Frontend: React + Tailwind  
- Database: PostgreSQL  
- Queue System: Redis / Celery
