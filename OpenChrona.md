# OpenChrona

### The Living Library of Clocks

OpenChrona is an AGPL-3.0+ open-source AI platform for exploring, designing, simulating, visualizing, and building every major form of timekeeping technology throughout human history.

From ancient sundials and water clocks to mechanical escapements, quartz oscillators, digital systems, and atomic synchronization, OpenChrona is designed to become a living educational and engineering ecosystem dedicated to the preservation and advancement of horology and timekeeping knowledge.

The platform combines:
- AI-assisted design,
- procedural mechanical generation,
- interactive simulations,
- historical archives,
- fabrication workflows,
- and modular educational systems

into a unified open-source environment for makers, engineers, educators, historians, researchers, and clock enthusiasts.

---

# Vision

OpenChrona exists to preserve, reconstruct, and expand humanity’s understanding of timekeeping systems through open collaboration, engineering simulation, and interactive learning.

The long-term goal is to create a complete living library of clocks where users can:
- study historical mechanisms,
- generate new designs,
- simulate real-world behavior,
- fabricate physical components,
- and contribute new discoveries back to the community.

# Design Philosophy

OpenChrona treats timekeeping as one of humanity’s most important engineering achievements.

The platform is intended not only to archive historical systems, but to make them understandable, reproducible, interactive, and expandable through open collaboration and modern computational tools.

By combining education, engineering, simulation, fabrication, and AI-assisted learning into one ecosystem, OpenChrona aims to become the definitive open-source platform for horology and timekeeping research.
