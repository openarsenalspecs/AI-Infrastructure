# Adaptive Materials Engine (AME)

**Designing systems that don’t need plastic.**

Adaptive Materials Engine (AME) is an open-source engineering intelligence system that helps engineers, makers, and designers replace plastic components with locally sourced, economically feasible, and mechanically equivalent or superior materials. When direct substitution is not possible, AME automatically redesigns the system to eliminate plastic dependency entirely.

AME is built as a **circular materials intelligence platform** combining material science reasoning, supply chain awareness, redesign automation, and lifecycle environmental optimization.

---

## 🌍 Core Purpose

AME exists to:

- Reduce and eliminate unnecessary plastic use in physical systems
- Enable real-world material substitution based on engineering constraints
- Optimize for local sourcing and reduced transport impact
- Ensure cost feasibility under strict economic limits
- Support redesign when materials cannot be directly replaced
- Promote circular economy engineering practices

---

# 🧠 Full Feature Set

## 🌍 1. Plastic Substitution Engine
- Decomposes any product into functional engineering requirements
- Identifies baseline plastic material (ABS, PP, Nylon, etc.)
- Recommends alternative materials:
  - metals and alloys
  - engineered polymers
  - natural composites
  - bio-based materials
- Ensures mechanical equivalence or improvement over plastic baseline

---

## 💰 2. Economic Feasibility Constraint System
- Hard constraint: **no recommendation above +15% total cost increase**
- Includes:
  - material cost
  - manufacturing cost
  - tooling and fabrication cost
  - transport and logistics cost
- Filters invalid recommendations automatically

---

## 🌍 3. Local Material Intelligence Network
- Prioritizes geographically local sourcing
- Maps regional material availability:
  - scrap yards
  - recycling centers
  - industrial surplus suppliers
  - hardware stores
- Builds availability heatmaps (high / medium / low)
- Tracks supplier reliability and consistency
- Reduces transport emissions and cost by default

---

## 🌐 4. Global vs Local Optimization Mode Switch
Selectable system-wide optimization modes:

- Local-First Mode (default)
- Global Optimization Mode
- Sustainability-Max Mode
- Cost-Min Mode

Each mode reweights material selection priorities dynamically.

---

## 🔧 5. Mechanical Validation System
- Validates material substitutions against engineering constraints:
  - tensile strength
  - fatigue resistance
  - thermal stability
  - chemical resistance
  - wear resistance
- Rejects unsafe or non-compliant substitutions

---

## 🏗️ 6. Redesign Auto-Generator
When substitution is not possible:
- Automatically redesigns component structure
- Replaces plastic-dependent architectures
- Adjusts manufacturing methods:
  - injection molding → CNC / casting / additive manufacturing
- Modifies assembly systems:
  - snap-fit → mechanical fasteners
- Reduces part count and improves modularity

---

## ♻️ 7. Circular Economy Impact Scoring
- Measures plastic reduction per recommendation
- Evaluates recyclability and biodegradability
- Estimates lifecycle environmental impact
- Compares baseline vs replacement design outcomes

---

## 🌱 8. Carbon Accounting Engine
- Estimates embodied carbon per material choice
- Includes:
  - production emissions
  - transport emissions
  - manufacturing energy use
- Outputs carbon savings or increases per substitution

---

## 🧠 9. Material Knowledge Graph
- Graph-based material intelligence system
- Links:
  - materials
  - properties
  - suppliers
  - manufacturing methods
- Enables relational reasoning:
  - replaceability
  - compatibility
  - redesign requirements

---

## 📊 10. Multi-Objective Constraint Solver
- Balances:
  - cost constraint (≤ +15%)
  - mechanical requirements
  - local availability
  - environmental impact
- Produces ranked optimized solutions

---

## 🧠 11. Circular Economy Feedback Loop
- Learns from real-world outcomes:
  - material performance
  - failure rates
  - supplier reliability
  - cost accuracy
- Dynamically updates scoring models over time

---

## 🧪 12. Experimental Materials Sandbox
- Includes emerging materials:
  - mycelium composites
  - hemp-based polymers
  - ocean plastic alternatives
- Clearly marked as experimental and non-guaranteed

---

## 🏭 13. Manufacturing Optimization System
- Selects optimal manufacturing method per material:
  - CNC machining
  - casting
  - forging
  - additive manufacturing
- Optimizes for local capability and cost efficiency

---

## 🧠 14. Engineering Mode System
Selectable reasoning modes:

- Mechanical Engineer Mode
- Industrial Designer Mode
- Sustainability Auditor Mode
- DIY Maker Mode
- Procurement Mode

---

## 🚫 15. Plastic Avoidance Design Layer
- Detects plastic-dependent designs
- Suggests non-plastic redesign before substitution
- Encourages structural alternatives at the design stage

---

## ⚠️ 16. Failure Mode Prediction System
- Predicts material failure risks:
  - creep
  - UV degradation
  - fatigue cracking
  - thermal deformation
- Estimates expected lifespan under conditions

---

## 📦 17. Product Decomposition Engine
- Breaks products into:
  - functional components
  - load distribution maps
  - material composition layers
- Enables granular substitution

---

## 🔄 18. Assembly Reconfiguration System
- Redesigns assemblies for new materials:
  - improves repairability
  - replaces permanent joints
  - reduces plastic fasteners
- Supports modular system design

---

## 🌐 19. Supply Chain Optimization Layer
- Minimizes:
  - transport distance
  - logistics emissions
- Prioritizes local material ecosystems

---

## 📉 20. Plastic Dependency Scoring System
- Scores designs from 0–100 based on:
  - plastic reliance
  - structural dependency
  - redesign complexity
- Enables benchmarking across industries

---

## 🧩 21. Design Pattern Library for Non-Plastic Engineering
- Reusable engineering templates:
  - metal hinge systems
  - wood joinery structures
  - modular mechanical systems
- Converts plastic-based designs into structural patterns
