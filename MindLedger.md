# MindLedger – Modular Business Management Platform

**Living Version**

> MindLedger is continuously evolving. Modules and features are updated as technology improves, open-source components are released, and laws change. The AI core ensures system integrity, compliance, security, and continuous improvement.

---

## Overview

MindLedger is a **fully modular, open-source business management platform** designed for service-based businesses, studios, salons, repair shops, professional services, and organizations requiring flexible CRM, scheduling, billing, compliance, and operational management.

Built around a modular architecture, MindLedger allows businesses to enable only the features they need while expanding functionality through optional plugin modules. The platform is designed to be customizable, privacy-focused, end-to-end encrypted, and continuously evolving.

The integrated AI assistant acts as the intelligent operating layer for MindLedger, allowing users to configure workflows, create custom processes, receive guidance, automate tasks, and maintain compliance through natural language and voice interaction.

---

# Core Architecture

MindLedger is built using a modular system design:

- Core modules provide the essential foundation of the platform.
- Optional plugin modules extend functionality for specific industries and advanced use cases.
- AI assistance connects all modules through natural language interaction.
- All modules are designed with open-source components.
- All sensitive information is protected through end-to-end encryption.
- Users maintain control over their data, workflows, and customization.
