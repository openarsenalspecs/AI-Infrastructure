# LocaleMaterials

**Where Geography Meets Material Science.**

LocaleMaterials is an open-source, modular AI platform that helps users design and build with **regenerative, locally sourced, climate-adaptive building materials**. It connects geography, ecology, supply chains, and construction science into a unified decision system that produces real, buildable outcomes—not just recommendations.

The goal is to shift construction from globalized supply dependence to **place-based material intelligence**.

---

# 🌍 Core Mission

To identify and generate the most:
- 🌱 Regenerative  
- 📍 Locally available  
- 🧾 Code-compliant  
- 💰 Economically efficient  
- 🌡️ Climate-resilient  

materials and construction systems for any location on Earth.

# 🧩 Modular System Architecture

LocaleMaterials is built as a **modular, open-source ecosystem**, allowing each component to operate independently or as part of a full pipeline.

---

## 🌍 Geo-Environmental Intelligence Layer
Analyzes local environmental conditions:
- Soil composition
- Climate patterns
- Hydrology and flood risk
- Native biomass availability
- Forestry and agricultural systems
- Regional waste streams

---

## 🧠 AI Material Recommendation Engine
Provides ranked material systems based on **LocaleScore™**, which evaluates:
- Regeneration rate  
- Embodied energy  
- Transport distance  
- Toxicity  
- Repairability  
- End-of-life reuse  
- Local economic impact  

---

## 📍 Local Supply Network Mapper
Maps real-world sourcing options:
- Local mills and producers
- Salvage yards and reuse centers
- Agricultural and fiber sources
- Regional and state supply chains

---

## 🌱 Climate Adaptation Optimizer
Adjusts recommendations based on:
- Long-term temperature shifts
- Flood risk changes
- Fire risk zones
- Humidity and mold pressure
- Freeze/thaw cycles

Outputs:
- Current optimal material choice  
- Long-term resilient alternative  
- Cost vs durability tradeoffs  

---

## 🧠 AI Design Co-Pilot (Early Stage Planning)
Helps shape the structure before materials are chosen:
- Suggests optimal building types for climate and terrain
- Improves passive heating/cooling design
- Reduces material demand through geometry optimization
- Recommends layout efficiency strategies

---

## 📍 Regional Material Ecosystem Maps
Interactive mapping of:
- Material abundance zones
- Industrial byproducts usable in construction
- Agricultural waste streams
- Supply gaps and dependency zones

---

## 🧪 Failure + Performance Database
A real-world learning system that tracks:
- Material failures by climate
- Construction mistakes
- Durability and aging outcomes
- Cost overruns
- Field-tested performance reports

---

## 🌪️ Disaster Rebuild Mode
Specialized emergency response system for:
- Hurricanes
- Floods
- Wildfires
- Earthquakes

Provides:
- Rapid material sourcing
- Salvage-based reconstruction paths
- Emergency shelter systems
- Transition plans to permanent regenerative builds

# ⚙️ Technical Stack

## Backend
- Python + FastAPI

- Deterministic sustainability scoring engine  
- Region-prioritized dataset weighting

- Progressive Web App (offline-capable)  
- Lightweight React or SvelteKit  
- Map-driven user interface  

## Data Sources
- Open soil and climate datasets  
- Forestry and agricultural data  
- Material supply chain databases  
- Community field reports
