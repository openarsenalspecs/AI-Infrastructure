# CarryChain

**Freight that Follows Fairness**

CarryChain is an open-source, non-speculative marketplace and logistics network that enables direct trade between producers and purchasers with fully transparent pricing and verified transport.

This platform removes unnecessary intermediaries, eliminates speculative trading, and introduces a fair, open infrastructure for global commerce.

---

## 🌐 Overview

CarryChain connects:

**Producer → Carrier → Buyer**

With:
- Transparent pricing  
- Verified participants  
- Open logistics  
- No speculation  

Built and maintained openly, CarryChain is designed to serve as **infrastructure for fair trade at global scale**.

---

## 🚀 Core Principles

- No speculative trading (no futures, no derivatives)  
- Direct producer-to-buyer transactions  
- Transparent pricing and logistics  
- Verified identity and compliance  
- Open-source and community-driven
