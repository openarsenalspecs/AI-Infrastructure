# OpenAudia Specification
**Own Your Media. Share Your World.**
- HTML Mirror:  [https://roxanneardary.com/openaudia-specification/](https://roxanneardary.com/openaudia-specification/)  

---

OpenAudia is an open source, modular media platform for ebooks, audiobooks, podcasts, and music. It is designed to give creators, readers, listeners, communities, organizations, and independent operators greater control over digital media publishing, distribution, discovery, licensing, and infrastructure.

OpenAudia supports self hosted deployment and is designed to operate as a configurable platform where installations can enable the capabilities they need without requiring every feature to be part of every deployment.

## Architectural Principles

### Modular Design

Core functionality is separated into modules with defined responsibilities and interfaces.

Optional functionality is implemented through plugins whenever practical.

### Self Hosting

Installations should be capable of operating independently without requiring a centralized OpenAudia service.

### Interoperability

OpenAudia should use established formats, protocols, APIs, and metadata standards whenever practical.

### Portability

Users and creators should be able to export their media, metadata, libraries, playlists, collections, and other supported data.

### Creator Control

Creators should have meaningful control over publication, visibility, licensing, downloading, streaming, embedding, and removal of their content.

### Accessibility

Accessibility should be considered throughout the platform rather than treated as an isolated feature.

### Privacy

Analytics and platform functionality should minimize unnecessary collection, retention, and exposure of personal information.

### Extensibility

The plugin system should allow installations to extend OpenAudia without modifying the core platform.

### Infrastructure Independence

OpenAudia should avoid unnecessary dependence on proprietary services and should support self hosted and open source alternatives.

## Content Rights

OpenAudia does not grant users ownership of media uploaded by other creators.

Creators and publishers remain responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, permissions, licenses, and authority to upload and distribute their content.

Installations may provide rights metadata, licensing information, attribution information, content ownership records, and removal workflows to support responsible publishing.

## Monetization Architecture

Monetization functionality is modular.

An installation may operate without monetization or may enable selected monetization capabilities.

Supported monetization models may include:

- Donations
- Tips
- Creator subscriptions
- Memberships
- Paid media
- Creator storefronts
- Sponsorships
- Advertising
- Marketplace transactions

Payment providers should be implemented through modular integrations rather than being required by the core platform.
